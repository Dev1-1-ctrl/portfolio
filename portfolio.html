About

I'm a First Class Computer Science graduate based in Cambridge. I finished school in Kerala, India, then studied at Anglia Ruskin University, where I worked on real client briefs and a final year project built on nearly half a million police records.

I enjoy the whole data journey: cleaning and validating messy data, training and evaluating models, and turning the results into something a non-technical person can understand. I'm also curious about SaaS businesses and how software companies use data to grow.

Outside coursework I build creative projects where code meets visual art: a hand-tracking comic filter, a generative poster tool and an AI photo toy. They're how I learn new tools quickly, because I want to see the result on screen.

I've worked in teams, led one, and run a large project on my own. Alongside my degree I worked part-time in hospitality, which taught me reliability and staying calm when it's busy. I speak English, Malayalam, Hindi and Tamil.
