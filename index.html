About Me

Where I'm from and what I enjoy.

I finished school in Kerala, then came to Cambridge for my degree and graduated from Anglia Ruskin with a First in 2026. Along the way I worked on real client briefs and a final year project built on nearly half a million police records.

My favourite part of a data project is the messy middle: cleaning up a dataset, working out whether a model is actually any good, and then explaining it so someone who doesn't code can use it. I'm also curious how SaaS companies use data to grow.

For fun I make odd little visual things, like the comic filter and the poster generator, and I doodle.

I worked part-time in hospitality through my degree, which is where I learned to stay calm when it gets busy. I speak English, Malayalam, Hindi and Tamil.
